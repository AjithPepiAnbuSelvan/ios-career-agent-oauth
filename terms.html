Usage information

iOS Career Agent is a personal utility maintained by its account owner. It is not offered as a public or paid service.

Audit output supports human review. A Gmail Sent record does not prove delivery, and a tracker entry does not prove an application was submitted. The owner reviews current vacancy details, duplicate history, and the latest resume before submitting any application.

Google and GitHub services remain subject to their respective terms and limits. No job offer or placement outcome is promised.
